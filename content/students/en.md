# If you do not know yet, we will teach you

Kicker: For Students

The laboratory is a place to start from the basics: we explain wireless communication ideas, help choose a task, and guide students toward a first concrete result.

## Enter the field

We explain the fundamentals of 5G/6G, coding, multiple access, and RIS in clear terms.

## Get a task

We help select a small research or engineering task that matches the student's current level.

## Produce a result

We support experiments, prototypes, talks, coursework, and graduation projects.

## Tracks

- Communication system modeling
- Coding and signal processing
- Network experiments
- Scientific writing and talks
