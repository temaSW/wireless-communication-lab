# Yakov Kryukov

Name: Yakov Kryukov
Role: Candidate of Technical Sciences, Associate Professor, one of the laboratory leads

Specialist in wireless communication systems, signal processing, and multiple access technologies. His professional work combines research, software model development, and education projects in telecommunications.

### Professional Profile

- 2025. Associate Professor at the Department of Telecommunications and Basic Principles of Radio Engineering, TUSUR.
- 2023-2028. Doctoral research on signal generation, processing, and transmission in multi-user wireless communication systems with power-domain channel separation and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces.

### Research Areas and Results

- Research areas: non-orthogonal multiple access, error-correcting coding, multi-antenna systems, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, and signal generation and processing for 5G/6G communication systems.
- Author and co-author of more than 100 research works; more than 60 publications are indexed in Scopus, Web of Science, the VAK list, and the Russian "White List".
- Author and co-author of 10 intellectual property results.

### Research Project Leadership

- RSF No. 21-79-00160 "Communication resource allocation in telecommunication systems with non-orthogonal multiple access", 2021-2023.
- RSF No. 24-29-00172 "Improving non-orthogonal multiple access communication systems using polar codes", 2023-2025.
- TUSUR projects under Priority 2030, the TUSUR Advanced Engineering School, and UMNIK related to PD-NOMA, polar codes, RIS, and STAR-RIS.

### Teaching

- Participates in TUSUR education since 2013.
- Supervised graduation theses for 31 bachelor's students and 18 master's students.
- Teaches seven communication-related courses in Russian and English; developed eight online courses.

### Awards

- Winner of the Vladimir Potanin Scholarship Program, 2017.
- Winner of the U-NOVUS young researchers' development competition, 2019.
- Second place in the TUSUR Best E-Course competition, 2023.
- Laureate of the Tomsk Region award in education, science, healthcare, and culture, 2024.
